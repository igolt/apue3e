**Q**: Run the program in Figure 4.9 after creating the files foo and bar. What happens?

---

**A**:
```bash
$ echo "this is foo" > foo
$ echo "this is bar" > bar
$ ls -lh foo bar
-rw-rw-r-- 1 igolt igolt 12 Apr 24 10:14 bar
-rw-rw-r-- 1 igolt igolt 12 Apr 24 10:14 foo
$ echo "=== foo content ==="; cat foo
=== foo content ===
this is foo
$ echo "=== bar content ==="; cat bar
=== bar content ===
this is bar
$ ./figure-4.9
$ ls -lh foo bar
-rw-rw-r-- 1 igolt igolt 0 Apr 24 10:17 bar
-rw-rw-r-- 1 igolt igolt 0 Apr 24 10:17 foo
$ echo "=== foo content ==="; cat foo
=== foo content ===
$ echo "=== bar content ==="; cat bar
=== bar content ===
```
Running the program in Figure 4.9 after creating the files foo and bar just
truncates the files preserving it's permission bits.
