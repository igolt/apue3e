**Q**: Is there any way for a function that is called by main to examine the
command line arguments without (a) passing `argc` and `argv` as arguments from
main to the function or (b) having main copy `argc` and `argv` into global
variables?

---

**A**: No, not portably. A function other than main has no standard way to
access `argc` and `argv` unless they are passed or made available through some
shared state. Operating systems may provide non-standard mechanisms, such as
Linux's `/proc/self/cmdline`.
